Dental Lab

Dental Lab Modernization Project

Our ground campus included a fully equipped dental lab with four functional X-ray machines. However, due to outdated hardware, missing drivers, and long-expired vendor contracts, the equipment had been unusable for years. Students were relying on hand‑drawn diagrams, copied images, and videos instead of gaining hands-on experience with real dental imaging tools.

I led the effort to restore the lab to full functionality. Some of the key steps included:

  • Identifying and sourcing compatible dental sensors for the existing X-ray equipment, and coordinating the purchase order.
  • Installing and configuring the correct drivers for the new sensors.
  • Setting up a dedicated server instance for Eaglesoft, the campus’s patient management software, and configuring four client stations—one for each X-ray device.
  • Updating and modifying firewall rules to ensure secure and reliable communication between devices and the server.

Thanks to these efforts, students are now able to perform full dental X-ray work, gaining real-world experience with professional imaging equipment.
