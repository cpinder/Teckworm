About Teckworm.com

With billions of webpages across the internet, I wanted to carve out a space that feels different — a corner that’s fun, informative, and genuinely helpful. Teckworm.com is my digital home for both the professional work I do and the personal interests that inspire me.

  • A resource for IT service management, Windows administration, and practical ITIL best practices
  • A home base for my business as it continues to grow and evolve
  • A meeting place for fellow IT professionals to connect, learn, and share ideas
  • A hub for the tools, links, and topics I’m into

Thanks for stopping by — I’m glad you found your way to this little slice of the web.

About Me & My Background

I work in IT as a Senior Service Desk Technician for a large medical training academy that serves both online and ground campuses. In my day-to-day role, I provide chat and phone support for our employees, helping them navigate technical issues with confidence and clarity.

As a senior technician, I handle more complex challenges, support my teammates as they resolve issues, and manage a wide range of operational responsibilities — including incident and request management, account administration, and access management. I take pride in being someone my team can rely on.

Outside of work, I’m married — going on 21 years — and a proud parent of two wonderful children. My family is the most important part of my life, and I’m grateful every day for the love, support, and joy they bring.
